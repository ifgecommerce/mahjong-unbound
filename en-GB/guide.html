Mahjong Unbound

Guide

Remove all tiles by matching free pairs. This is Mahjong Solitaire, not four-player Mahjong.

A tile is free if it is not covered, even partly, and at least its left or right side is open.

Standard tiles match identical symbols. Any flower matches another flower; any season matches another season. Flowers do not match seasons.

Every starting arrangement has a verified solution. Not every available pair preserves it: you can use Undo.

Size is the number of tiles, not a guaranteed difficulty. You can zoom and move around the board.

Help is free. Assisted results are labelled separately, with no paid penalties.

The challenge depends on the date and rules version. No daily downloads are needed.

Progress is local and does not sync between devices. Uninstalling the app may delete it.

The game works offline. Buying or restoring Pro may need a connection and an Apple account.

No IFG account, ads or ad tracking. Games and statistics stay on your device.

Pro is a one-time purchase for this app. It does not unlock other IFG apps.
