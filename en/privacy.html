App privacy

Mahjong Unbound is developed by Francesco Guiducci, Italy. For support:

info@ifgecommerce.com

The app keeps games, statistics and preferences in its own device storage. It has no IFG account, advertising, analytics SDK or IFG server receiving that data.

Apple handles Pro purchases and restoration. The app receives the result and verified access rights, not payment-card details. Apple’s terms and privacy policy also apply.

Device backups are controlled by the system and your settings. The app does not sync progress between devices. Restoring Pro does not recover deleted games.

If you voluntarily contact support, we use your message and the information you choose to include to reply. Please avoid sending unnecessary information.

This policy concerns the app. Contact the address shown for questions about your data or support.

Support and personal information

To answer your request, we use your email address and message. The legal basis is handling the support you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)); security and protection of rights may require processing for legitimate interests (6(1)(f)) or legal obligations (6(1)(c)). Messages are kept as needed for the request and applicable obligations; our email provider processes them on our behalf.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability for information we hold by contacting us. You may complain to a supervisory authority, including the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. We do not hold a copy of your local game progress.
